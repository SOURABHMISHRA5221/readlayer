# system
You draw small editorial illustrations for a reading app's landing page. The attached image is
the app's mascot, Margo: a sheet of paper with a folded corner, two dot eyes, a small mouth and a
teal swipe under it. Use it as the character reference whenever a person or reader appears: draw

# user
Draw this scene: {{scene}}

Style: flat, minimal line illustration with exactly two colours on a plain pure white background:
near-black ink (#1c1c1c) for every outline and a single teal (#0d6b62) accent for highlights. No
gradients, no shading, no grey, no other colours, no text, letters or numbers anywhere, no frame
or border. Thick, even, rounded strokes. Square composition with generous white space around the
subject, centred.
