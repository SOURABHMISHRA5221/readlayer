Bond yields fall as markets price a long pause

The Reserve Bank of India kept its repo rate unchanged at 6.5% on Wednesday, the tenth straight meeting without a move, as retail inflation eased to 3.9% in August, the lowest in five years.

Bond markets had largely priced in the pause, and the yield on the benchmark 10-year government bond fell three basis points to 6.42%, flattening the yield curve between two-year and ten-year maturities.

The monetary policy committee also kept the cash reserve ratio, the share of deposits that banks must park with the central bank, at 4%, and held its forecast for real GDP growth this year at 6.8%.

Economists said a cut before March would need a sharper fall in food prices, and warned that a weaker rupee could keep imported inflation high even as fuel costs ease through the winter.
