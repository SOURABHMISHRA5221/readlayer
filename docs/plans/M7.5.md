# M7.5 plan: "For you" feed, "Read any link", reading goals; general explanation prompts

Status: DRAFT (plan-reviewer round 1 pending).
Decisions: 33 (M7.5 scope), 35 (growth for any reader), 37 (generalise explanation prompts here).
Paths: `app/`, `scripts/`, `evals/` are under `apps/api/`; `data/` is at the repo root.

## Context

M2.2 made ReadLayer open-domain: 14 domains, a seeded graph (5,206 concepts) and online growth,
live since 2026-10-10. A Google reader can now pick health and sports at onboarding, but the
library is a business corpus and there is no way in except "Open here" from a business article.
M7.5 gives signed-in readers their own way in (decision 33):

1. a **For you** feed per interest topic (articles and podcasts from Tavily, videos from
   YouTube), ranked for the reader;
2. a visible **Read any link** box on the library;
3. **reading goals** (habit and learning) shown on the profile and the knowledge map.

It also generalises the explanation prompts, which still say "business and economy news"
(decision 37), so a sports or health article gets explanations framed for its own field.

**Demo readers:** BUILD_PLAN scopes the feed and goals to signed-in readers. Demo personas keep
the library and Up next. Read any link works for every reader within the existing caps (decision
35 already lets demo readers grow concepts from a pasted link).

## A. For you feed (API)

1. **Topics.** A Google reader's interest topics are the taxonomy domains they picked at
   onboarding (or later raised in the profile), at most 6. No interests → the four business
   domains. Demo readers get 403 `feed_signed_in_only`.
2. **Search, shared and cached per (topic, format, UTC day).**
   - The only query text is the taxonomy label plus a fixed format phrase:
     - article: `"<Topic> latest news"` (Tavily, `topic: news`, last 7 days);
     - podcast: `"<Topic> podcast episode"` (Tavily on the podcast domains);
     - video: `"<Topic> explained"` (YouTube).
   - Never reader text, history or concept names.
   - Cache key `feed:<domain>:<format>:<YYYY-MM-DD>` in the existing `rec_cache` collection. It
     is shared by every reader; stale entries are never served the next day.
   - The existing blocklist and the `mentions(topic)` relevance gate apply, plus a stricter
     article filter: published within 14 days when Tavily gives a date.
3. **Caps and budget.**
   - New `FEED_SEARCH_DAILY_CAP` (default 60 calls/day, all readers). The feed's searches also
     count against the existing `SEARCH_DAILY_CAP` (300) and the YouTube cap, so the feed can
     never starve Up next.
   - Worst case 14 topics × 3 formats = 42 searches (YouTube 2 calls each) = 56 calls/day.
   - Tavily ≈ 1 credit and YouTube ≈ 101 quota units per search; no LLM anywhere in the feed.
   - A cap hit serves yesterday's cache if present, else an honest empty state ("Fresh picks
     arrive tomorrow").
4. **Fetch pattern (as Up next).**
   - GET `/v1/feed` returns cached items at once and starts background refreshes for missing
     (topic, format, day) entries, with `pending: true`. The client re-asks after 3 s, up to 3
     times.
   - Cloud Run throttles CPU after a response, so the first reader of the day may see a partly
     filled feed until the re-ask. Same trade-off as Up next today (STATUS §4).
5. **Ranking** (`app/recs/feed_rank.py`, pure, no I/O).
   `score = interest(topic) × format_affinity(format) × readiness × goal_boost × freshness`:
   - `interest`: the reader's domain interest (1.0 chosen, 0.3 otherwise).
   - `format_affinity`: the existing per-reader format model (`rank.format_affinity`, rec
     signals).
   - `readiness`, deterministic, no LLM:
     - find the graph concepts the item names, by exact label/alias match over 1–4-word n-grams
       of its title + snippet;
     - count those whose `p_know` for this reader is below the annotation threshold (new to
       them);
     - k new: 1–4 → 1.0; 0 → 0.6 (nothing new to learn); k > 4 → 0.8^(k−4) (too much at once).
   - `goal_boost`: 1.3 if the item names a learning-goal target concept (§C), else 1.0.
   - `freshness`: the existing `rank.freshness`.
   - Selection: format quotas 3 articles / 2 videos / 1 podcast per topic, then interleaved by
     score, at most 24 items. Each item carries a `why` line, e.g. "Health · 2 ideas new to you
     · helps your goal: understand insulin resistance".
6. **Opening an item.** Articles go through the existing POST `/v1/recs/open` (two-phase "Open
   here", ingest caps, growth slots). Videos and podcasts open the original in a new tab. Every
   open, shown and dismiss reuses POST `/v1/recs/events` (feed items get ids `feed:<hash>`), so
   format affinity learns from the feed too.
7. **Privacy.** The feed reads the reader's interests, θ and knows in memory and sends nothing
   about the reader anywhere. Search calls carry only topic labels; rec signals already sit in
   `users/{uid}/rec_signals` (export/delete covered).

## B. Read any link

- A labelled URL field at the top of the library: "Read any link — paste an article". It calls
  POST `/v1/recs/open` (two-phase, `user_ingested`, per-user and shared anonymous ingest caps,
  growth slots) and navigates to `/read/{id}` at once, with the article showing within ~5 s and
  notes after.
- Errors use the existing messages: unsafe URL, robots, not an article, cap reached (with the
  sign-in hint for demo readers).
- Works for demo readers too (decision 35). A test proves the box sends the URL and nothing else.

## C. Reading goals

1. **Store.** `users/{uid}/goals/{goal_id}`, a new user subcollection added to
   `USER_SUBCOLLECTIONS` (export and delete, test). Google readers only. At most 5 goals.
2. **Habit goal.** `{type: habit, metric: articles | minutes, per_week: 1–50 | 10–600}`.
   Progress = this ISO week's `weekly` doc: articles = `len(content_ids)`, minutes =
   `words_read / 230`. Deterministic.
3. **Learning goal.** `{type: learning, text}`, where the text is "understand X", ≤ 120 chars.
   - The text is redacted (`app/privacy/redact.py`) and the "understand / learn / get"
     prefixes are stripped.
   - X resolves through `resolve_names` (exact match, then embedding candidates plus
     `link_confirm`, the linker's own path). The prompt sees the redacted phrase only, never
     reading history (same rule as profile edits).
   - Targets = the concept plus its prerequisite chain, most basic first, at most 8. Stored as
     ids plus labels.
   - Unresolvable text → 422 `goal_not_found` ("We couldn't find that idea yet — try another
     name"), nothing stored.
   - Cap `GOAL_RESOLVE_DAILY_CAP` 10 per reader per day (one `link_confirm` call at most each).
4. **Learning progress** = targets at band "understands" or "can explain" / total, computed on
   read from `knows` and the prior. No LLM.
5. **API.** GET `/v1/me/goals` (with progress), POST `/v1/me/goals`, DELETE
   `/v1/me/goals/{id}`. Consent required, as for other signed-in writes.
6. **Feed boost** as §A.5. The knowledge map marks goal targets (a ring) and offers a "Goal:
   X" filter showing just those nodes.

## D. Web

1. **Library header:** "Library · For you" tabs for Google readers. The For you page is

   - topic sections, item cards (reusing Up next's card with format icon, source, duration and

   - empty and pending states;
   - keyboard and screen-reader usable.
2. **Read any link** field on the library for every reader.
3. **Profile:** a Goals section (add habit, add learning, delete, progress bars with text
   values) and the map's goal ring and filter.
4. **Design critique** (desktop, mobile, dark) on For you, the library box and Goals; axe 0; no
   horizontal scroll at 390.

## E. General explanation prompts (decision 37)

1. `explain` v5, `restate` v2, `verify` v3 and `verify_background` v2: "business and economy
   news" becomes news on any subject, and examples gain one non-business case each. No
   held-out wording. The output schemas don't change.
2. **Before/after on the frozen safety cases (decision 26).**
   - "Before" is the shipped `explain-v4-split` row in `evals/report.md`, re-run only if the
     runner changed since.
   - "After" is one full safety run on the same cases.
   - Targets as M5: every adversarial card rejected, ≥ 90% good cards accepted, all injections
     safe. A miss stops the switch.
   - ≈ $2.
3. **Cache.** The new versions change the explanation cache keys, so every cached card misses
   once. `scripts/warm_demo.py` re-warms the three demo articles × 3 personas after the deploy
   (a production write: ≈ 27 Backgrounds and ≈ 15 restatements, ≈ $0.25).
